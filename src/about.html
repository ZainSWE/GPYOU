About GPYOU

The Problem

The GPU market is overwhelming.

GPYOU is a GPU recommendation tool designed to help users find the right graphics card based on their needs, preferences, and budget.

The graphics card market is constantly changing with dozens of models, confusing specifications, and widely varying prices make it difficult to know what to choose. GPYOU simplifies that process by asking you a few key questions, then offering personalized recommendations tailored to your situation.

Whether you're building a new PC, upgrading an old system, or trying to understand which GPU offers the best value. GPYOU helps you make an informed decision without needing deep technical knowledge.

What We Offer

Built for everyone.

  • Clean, distraction-free interface
  • Simple prompts for gaming, creative work, and development
  • Manufacturer and budget preferences
  • AI-powered real-time matching
  • 3D model viewer for every GPU
  • No brand bias, no outdated benchmarks

Our Mission

Clarity in a crowded market.

GPYOU is built for anyone, from first-time builders to experienced users who wants a faster, cleaner path to the right GPU.

We combine a curated dataset with AI reasoning so every recommendation is grounded in real specs and real prices, not guesswork.
